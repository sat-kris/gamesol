# Stillz

Games that build your attention instead of stealing it.

## Put it online with GitHub Pages (free, about 5 minutes)

1. Sign in at github.com (create a free account if you need one).
2. Click **+** (top right) > **New repository**. Name it `stillz`, choose **Public**, click **Create repository**.
3. On the new repo page, click **uploading an existing file**. Drag in all the files from this folder
   (`index.html`, `manifest.webmanifest`, `icon.svg`, `icon-180.png`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `README.md`). Click **Commit changes**.
4. Go to **Settings** > **Pages**. Under "Build and deployment", set Source to **Deploy from a branch**,
   Branch to **main** and folder **/ (root)**. Click **Save**.
5. Wait 1 to 2 minutes and refresh the Pages settings. Your link appears at the top:
   `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/stillz/`

Share that link on WhatsApp. Anyone can play, no sign-in needed.

## Tips

- **Install like an app:** on the phone, open the link, then "Add to Home Screen" (iPhone: Share button; Android: browser menu).
- **Your cockpit:** open `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/stillz/#cockpit` or tap the stillz logo 5 times. It shows usage on that device.
- **Updating:** upload a new `index.html` to the repo; the site refreshes within a couple of minutes.
- **Privacy:** no accounts, ads or tracking. Scores stay in each player's own browser.
