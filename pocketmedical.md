## Game mapping (summary)
- ADHD: Opposite, Rule Switch, Quick Draw, Three Cups, Chaos, Bloom/Breathe/Settle, Sequence, Vanish
- Autism: Pairs, Tic-Tac-Toe, Four in a Row, Sudoku 4x4, Towers, Slide, Maze, Spot the Difference, Breathe (sound off, Easy)
- Dyslexia: Glide, Twin Lanes, Chaos, Air Hockey, Sound Memory
- Dyscalculia: Estimate, Sums, Tally, Quick Sort
- Motor (DCD/CP): one-tap games — Glide, Stack, Bullseye, Beat, Whack; turn-based board games
- Intellectual disability: Pairs, Whack, Sums, Sudoku Starter, Tic-Tac-Toe on Easy
- Epilepsy: safety only. Full-screen tap flashes in Bullseye, Opposite, Rule Switch (red/green), Clash (mint). Fast tapping can exceed 3/sec. Proposed fix: remove or soften full-screen flashes.
